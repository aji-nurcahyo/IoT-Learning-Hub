## 2. Anti-Slop Writing & Structural Audit

In accordance with `AGENTS.md` and the permanent `antislop active: during` mode:
1. **Tone:** Natural academic Indonesian with English technical engineering terms preserved (`Power Saving Mode`, `Coverage Enhancement`, `Ray-Casting`, `Point-in-Polygon`, `Envelope Analysis`).
2. **Conciseness:** Zero filler phrases ("In today's fast-paced digital world", "Needless to say", "Delve into the wonders of").
3. **Mermaid & Diagrams:** Clean SVG diagrams with consistent color tokens (#0f172a slate background, #38bdf8 sky blue, #a855f7 purple, #10b981 emerald green) rendering responsive technical diagrams across both desktop and mobile viewports.
4. **Slide Deck Alignment:** Slide presentations contain high-signal bulleted concepts, technical takeaways, and speaker notes rather than conversational paragraphs.
